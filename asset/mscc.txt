MOUNTAIN SUITES AND COUNTRY CLUB (MSCC)
1. What is the name of the project and who is the developer?
The project is called Mountain Suites and Country Club. It is developed by
VHermosa Bright Corporation (VHBC).
2. Where is it located?
Inside Mountain View Leisure Community, Brgy. Munting Indang, Nasugbu,
Batangas.
3. What is the project about/what is its concept?
A serene, low-density residential condominium and condotel within MVLC,
thoughtfully designed for refined countryside living and complemented by full
resort-style amenities while also being near stunning beaches, truly a dual lifestyle
advantage. It is perfectly positioned for both personal leisure and hassle-free
income generation, offering a beautiful location paired with world-class facilities and
amenities.
4. What are the location features?
MSCC enjoys the same refreshing highland climate and breathtaking scenery as
MVLC. Nestled in a peaceful, elevated setting near Tagaytay, it offers sweeping
views of both mountains and the ocean; perfectly situated at the very peak of the
mountain.
5. What are the amenities featured?
Resort-type swimming pool, wellness and leisure spaces, floral garden, chapel,
helipad, grand lobby, and 360° mountain and sea views.
6. How big is the project/what are its specifications?
The whole MSCC development spans approximately 14,000 sqm with 2 towers,
each having 6 floors and 135 units.
7. Who will build my unit? Can I bring my own contractor?
Units are delivered by the in-house contractor as per the buyer’s chosen finish
(Fully-furnished, semi-furnished, and bare units). Refer to Annex B for unit type
inclusions. Third-party contractors are allowed for fit-outs, provided they comply
with the building’s rules and regulations.
8. How wide are the roads inside the community?
MSCC shares the same internal road system as MVLC: Main roads are 16 meters,
collector roads 12 meters, and secondary roads 8–10 meters.
9. What utilities are available?
All basic utilities are provided, including electricity, water, drainage, etc. Internet
connection and phone signals are also accessible in the community.
10. How much are the association/condo dues?
₱80/sqm (starting rate).
11. Is property management available?
Yes. VHBC has an in-house property management team. Hassle-free services
(cleaning, leasing, marketing, and other related services) will start upon turnover of
the property.
